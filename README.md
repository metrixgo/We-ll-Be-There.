You are a normal student. You made a mistake. A mistake that you need to pay back.

You might've got away for now. But they will be here. To find you.

## Plot

**We'll Be There.** is a psychological horror game currently in development. It is inspired by one of my dreams in middle school. All you need to ruin one's life is a simple accident. Before you realize it, it's already over for you.
<img width="1669" height="760" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c0d33d3f-0564-4116-97b9-8d7aaed08838" />

In this game, the protagonist accidentally killed an important figure on the way home. You would need to identify the traces you left and clean up all evidence that will get you arrested.
<img width="2878" height="1380" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6972e1c3-ab7c-46e8-b6b7-94b3e18d39cc" />

After you managed to hide everything from the police, you've decided to relax. Except something is happening behind the scene. You can feel it. But you can't see it. The figure from your dream became true. It lures you deep into the ground, where you will explore more about what's happening, and eventually fall into an inevitable ending.
<img width="810" height="388" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f648f4ca-1ab5-44ad-8181-08f1ba5e173a" />

## Current Features
- 2 endings
- Retro atmosphere
- Jumpscares
- Realistic sound effects
- 40+ minute game play
- Clue-finding
- Abstract dreams
- Supernatural figures
- Supports English and Chinese
- Them

## Controls

WASD/Arrow keys for movement

Space/Enter/LMB for skipping dialogues

Left Mouse Button for the marjority of interactions

[F] for flashlight, [Shift] for sprint (Only when the game prompts you)
